HOW TO PLAY
// A Guide to Deception & Survival //
The Premise
A deadly secret hides in plain sight. Hidden among the citizens of your town are cold-blooded killers — the Mafia. Every night they strike. Every morning the survivors must debate, accuse, and vote someone out. Find the killers before they outnumber the innocent.
1
Setup
Host creates a room and invites players (4–12 players). The host configures how many of each special role to include, then deals secret role cards.
2
Night Falls
All players "close their eyes." Special roles are summoned one by one in secret to perform their night actions.
3
Dawn — Open Discussion
The night's casualties are revealed. Survivors debate and accuse each other.
4
The Vote
The town votes to eliminate one suspected player. Their role is revealed.
5
Repeat
Night falls again. The cycle continues until one side wins.

Day Phase
☀️
Reveal & Discuss
Night deaths are announced. Players debate freely.
⚖️
Vote
Everyone votes simultaneously. Most votes = eliminated. Tie = no kill.
⭐ Sheriff's Shot: Can only be used ONCE per game. Happens before the vote.
💘 Lover deaths: When a linked Lover dies, their partner dies immediately.
Win Conditions
🏙️
Town Wins
All Mafia members are eliminated.
🔪
Mafia Wins
The number of living Mafia equals or exceeds living Town members.
🃏
Jester Wins
The Jester is voted out by the town. Instant solo victory.
🧠 Town Strategy
Track who defends who. Mafia often protect their allies.
Detectives should reveal findings gradually to avoid being targeted.
Doctors: protect the Detective or Sheriff.
Watch for who's quiet — Mafia players often lay low.
🔪 Mafia Strategy
Accuse confidently. Passive Mafia get detected.
Kill the Detective before they out you.
Fake-claim a Town role if cornered — stay consistent.
🃏 Jester Strategy
Act suspicious — but not stupidly so.
Change your story slightly. Inconsistency triggers accusations.
